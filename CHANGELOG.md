## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-28

First published version.

- 876 exercises in English and French, with pictures, instructions and the muscles worked; your own
  exercises with a photo, video or GIF; your version of a library exercise.
- Exercise list filtered by muscle group, by muscles picked on a body figure, by the equipment at hand or
  none, and searched by name.
- Workouts with supersets, folders, warm-ups following the working sets, sides for exercises done one side
  at a time; shared as a file to another Halko server.
- Guided sessions with prefilled values, rest timer and stopwatch; plate and 1RM calculators.
- History, personal records and progress charts.
- English or French, kilograms or pounds, kilometres or miles, customisable colours.
- The theme follows the system's light or dark appearance by default; text follows the text size chosen
  on the iPhone, iPad or in the browser; "Increase contrast" and screen readers are taken into account.
- Real-time sync between devices, working without network; export and import of all your data.
- Works over HTTPS or plain HTTP, from any device on the network; HTTPS is advised.
- Docker image for linux/amd64, linux/arm64 and linux/arm/v7 (Windows and Mac through Docker Desktop,
  Raspberry Pi).
